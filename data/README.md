# Data

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `fake_job_postings.csv` | **EMSCAD**, from Kaggle's "Real / Fake Job Posting Prediction" (CC0; Employment Scam Aegean Dataset, University of the Aegean). 17,880 job listings from 2012–2014, 866 labeled `fraudulent`. Don't edit this file: the split checks its SHA-256 hash. |
| `fake_job_postings_cleaned.csv` | The same listings after cleaning (17,599 rows after removing exact duplicates), with parsed salary and location and the fixed split (`group_id`, `split`, `cv_fold`). Regenerated by `python -m ml.split`; don't edit by hand. |

What the cleaning does, and why the split is by company, is in [`ml/AUDIT.md`](../ml/AUDIT.md).

The label marks **scam postings**. It is not a measure of whether a genuine role gets filled.

Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shivamb/real-or-fake-fake-jobposting-prediction
